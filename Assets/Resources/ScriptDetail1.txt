ACT 3 SCENE 5

<u>Lady Capulet</u>
Find thou the means, and I'll find such a man. 
But now I'll tell thee joyful tidings, girl.

<i><size=75%>You find a suitable poison, and I’ll find a suitable servant.
But now I have to tell you good news, girl.</size></i>

<u>Juliet</u>
And joy comes well in such a needy time.
What are they, beseech your ladyship?

<i><size=75%>Joy is badly needed in this time. Please, 
mother, what is the good news?</size></i>

<u>Lady Capulet</u>
Well, well, thou hast a careful father, child;
One who, to put thee from thy heaviness,
Hath sorted out a sudden day of joy,
That thou expects not, nor I looked not for.

<i><size=75%>Well, you have a thoughtful father, child. Your father, 
to alleviate your grief, has arranged a joyful day for 
you, which neither you nor I anticipated.</size></i>

<u>Juliet</u>
Madam, in happy time. What day is that?

<i><size=75%>What day is that, mother?</size></i>

<u>Lady Capulet</u>
Marry, my child, early next Thursday morn
The gallant, young, and noble gentleman,
The County Paris, at Saint Peter's Church,
Shall happily make thee there a joyful bride.

<i><size=75%>Indeed, my child, it’s next Thursday in the early morning,
when Count Paris, the gallant, young, and noble gentleman, 
will make you a happy bride at Saint Peter’s Church.</size></i>

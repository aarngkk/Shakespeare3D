ACT 3 SCENE 5

<u>Juliet</u>
Good father, I beseech you on my knees,
Hear me with patience but to speak a word.

<i><size=75%>Good father, I’m begging you on my knees. 
Please just listen to me for a few words.</size></i>

<u>Capulet</u>
Hang thee, young baggage. Disobedient wretch!
I tell thee what. Get thee to church a Thursday,
Or never after look me in the face.

<i><size=75%>Damn you, useless girl! Disobedient wretch! I’ll tell you what. 
Get yourself to the church on Thursday or never 
look me in the face again.</size></i>

Speak not, reply not, do not answer me.
My fingers itch. 

<i><size=75%>Don’t speak to me, don’t reply, don’t answer. 
I feel like I could hit you.</size></i> 

Wife, we scarce thought us blessed
That God had lent us but this only child;
But now I see this one is one too much,
And that we have a curse in having her.
Out on her, hilding!

<i><size=75%>My wife, we thought we had been meagerly blessed having just this 
one child, but now I see that this one alone is too much, 
and we’re cursed to have her. Curse you, you beast!</size></i>

<u>Nurse</u>
God in heaven bless her!
You are to blame, my lord, to rate her so.

<i><size=75%>God in heaven bless her! You’re wrong, my lord, 
to yell at her like that.</size></i>

<u>Capulet</u>
And why, my lady wisdom? Hold your tongue,
Good Prudence, smatter with your gossips, go.

<i><size=75%>Why, are you the Lady of Wisdom? Hold your tongue. 
Go talk to your fellow gossips somewhere, go.</size></i>

<u>Nurse</u>
I speak no treason.

<i><size=75%>I said nothing wrong.</size></i>

<u>Capulet</u>
O, God ye good e'en.

<i><size=75%>Oh God, good afternoon already!</size></i>

<u>Nurse</u>
May not one speak?

<i><size=75%>Am I not allowed to speak?</size></i>

<u>Capulet</u>
Peace, you mumbling fool!
Utter your gravity o'er a gossip's bowl,
For here we need it not.

<i><size=75%>Quiet, you mumbling fool! Go spread your wise 
advice to some gossip, we don’t need that here.</size></i>

<u>Lady Capulet</u>
You are too hot.

<i><size=75%>You’re too angry.</size></i>
